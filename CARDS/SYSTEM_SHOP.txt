== SYSTEM SHOP ==

[SYSTEM] [SHOP]: ON
Current SP: 340

Multiplier (cashback): OFF
Multiplier value: 1.5

Categories available:
[STATS]  [SKILLS]  [PERKS]  [POWERS]  [ITEMS]

Usage:
- Type [SHOP] or [SYSTEM] to open the shop UI (suspends normal narration).
- Type [STATS] or any category in brackets to list that category's items.
- /shop : show shop status
- /shop list <CATEGORY> : list items in category
- /shop buy <CATEGORY> <item name> : purchase an item (SP deducted; effects must be applied by the scenario)

Notes:
- SP is tracked under state.aetherheart.shop.sp.
- Multiplier (cashback) gives immediate SP back on purchase when enabled. Configure here.
- Items are defined in the shop state and can be edited by the author for balance.
