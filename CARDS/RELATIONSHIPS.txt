== RELATIONSHIP WEB (LIVING DRAMA) ==

Tracks interpersonal opinions between characters (state.aetherheart.relationships).

Commands:
/relations - show all relationships
/relation set <A> <B> <value> - set A's opinion of B (-100 .. 100)
/relation adjust <A> <B> <delta> - adjust A's opinion of B by delta

Notes:
- Opinions are directional: A -> B may differ from B -> A.
- InfluenceTick propagates opinion weakly across the network and is exposed as AetherheartRelationships.influenceTick(hours) for integration with tickers/time.
- To allow a character to receive opinions, ensure there's a story card with the exact title matching the character name.
