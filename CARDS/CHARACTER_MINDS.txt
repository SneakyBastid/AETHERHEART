CHARACTER MINDS

Tracked characters: Father Aldric, Sister Maren, Captain Voss

Notes:
- This card configures which characters are tracked by the Character Minds subsystem.
- Use the 'Tracked characters:' line (comma-separated) to list character card titles that should accept memories.
- For a character to receive memories, its own story card must exist and its first non-empty line must be the activation marker:
  {memories: ON}
- Memories are appended to the character card's notes field exactly as:
  {title: <CharacterName>} — <single-line memory text>
- No timestamps, no metadata, and no fallback storage are used.
