🐉🔮 AETHERHEART — FULL MANUAL 🔮🐉

WHAT IS THIS?
Aetherheart is a big toolbox for AI Dungeon.
Each tool is a system you can turn ON or OFF.
If it’s OFF, it doesn’t bother you.
If it’s ON, it helps the story keep track of things
(time, money, health, quests, memories, and more).

You do not need all of it.
Start with Configure Aetherheart.
Flip on only what your story needs.

────────────────────────────────
1. THE MASTER SWITCH
Card: Configure Aetherheart

This is the control panel.
- Preset = what kind of world (fantasy, western, modern, cyberpunk, sci-fi, wasteland…)
- Rating = how mature the story may be (E, Teen, NSFW, Unrated)
- Rows of ON/OFF for big systems

Change a line from OFF to ON.
On the next turn, Aetherheart creates the cards for that system.

────────────────────────────────
2. TIME ⏰
Keeps a calendar and clock.
Each turn can move minutes or hours (Time/Turn).
Weather and seasons can follow the calendar.
Holidays can be listed; on that day the story knows it’s a holiday.
Schedule lets you set blocks like “Monday breakfast 7:00–7:55”.
Priority: Holiday beats a normal day; a specific day beats “every weekday”.

You can also put hours on a location card:
{hours: 6:00 AM | 5:00 PM}
And shifts on a character card:
{shift: Tavern | 8:00 PM | 2:00 AM}

────────────────────────────────
3. MONEY 💰
Currency name and symbol live on the Configure card.
Ledger remembers what you gained and spent.
You can say things like “buy a sword for 50” or “check my balance”.
Menu/price lists appear when you check prices or look at a menu
(story pauses and shows a list that fits the place you’re in).
Drugs you configure on the main card also show up in Prices & Goods.

────────────────────────────────
4. STUFF YOU CARRY 🎒
Inventory is split so cards don’t get huge:
- Clothes
- Armor
- Weapons
- Ammo
- Other

Armor and weapons are permanent by default (#).
You can mark anything permanent with #.
Only total weight matters for “are you overloaded?”
Overloaded can tire you out.

Say: give, take, equip, unequip, reload, wear, remove.

────────────────────────────────
5. BODY & SURVIVAL ❤️
Vitals: HP, MP, Stamina
Survival: Hunger, Thirst, Tiredness

Resting can restore you and push time forward.
Some magic uses MP (sorcery).
Some magic is words only (wizarding) — good for Harry Potter style.

Injuries can be recorded and slowly fade.
Bad ones may leave scars.

────────────────────────────────
6. GROWING STRONGER ⭐
Leveling tracks XP and level.
Skills, Abilities, Powers can be listed and added.
Loot can get stronger names as you level
(Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic).
The strong word always stays at the front of the item name.

d20: say “Roll for Attack” or “Roll for Saving” etc.
Stat % modifiers can boost those rolls.

────────────────────────────────
7. FIGHTING & PRESSURE ⚔️
Ammo is used when you shoot or reload.
Momentum rises when you succeed and falls when you fail
(optional light combat feel).
Deus Ex = intentional god mode (everything bends for you).
Anti-Deus Ex = no cheap sudden rescues when you want it hard.

────────────────────────────────
8. BOUNTIES, JOBS, QUESTS 📜
Bounty Board = wanted posters (dead / alive / either).
Taking a bounty can create a Quest automatically.
Job Board = normal work (deliveries, shifts, shop work).
Talk normally: “check the bounty board”, “take the job”, “turn in the bounty”.

────────────────────────────────
9. REPUTATION & JAIL ⚖️
Law / Social / Heat can move with your deeds.
Heat can fade slowly.
Jail can use the time system (hours tick down).
Bounties on you are optional.

────────────────────────────────
10. MEMORIES OF PEOPLE 🧠💭
Inner Thoughts
- Short memories about someone
- Stored in that card’s Notes
- Uses a hidden tag {title: Name} so the script knows which card
- Stable version of the old auto-memory idea

Inner Opinions
- How people feel about each other
- Overall vibe of the chapter
- Favors, debts, secrets, social pressure
- The “living characters” social stuff that is NOT raw memory

Player Journal
- Your adventure in short lines
- Example: “You got engaged.”
- Example: “The dragon was defeated by you.”

────────────────────────────────
11. MAKING CARDS 🃏
Card Creator only creates cards.
It can guess Character / Location / Other.
You can also type:
/card Character Bob tall scarred hunter

Format cards (_Character_, _Location_, _Other_) show the field layout.
You may edit those formats.

────────────────────────────────
12. HOW THE STORY SOUNDS 🎭
Narration controls:
- Does the AI write your character or only NPCs?
- Present or past tense
- Pacing and “don’t use bad AI habits”

Story Splices = flavor only (Western, Noir, Sitcom, etc.).
They do not change the plot.
Speech: Ebonics (or another pattern) on a character card
makes that character talk that way.

Special devices:
- Confessional Cutaway = private “talk to the reader” then snap back
- Deadpool Break = in-scene fourth wall (player), with cooldown
- Bombastic Superhero = pose on big declarations
- Live Studio Audience = laughs/gasps as flavor

────────────────────────────────
13. BIG STORY MOVES 🌀
Sleeve Jump = new body, same mind (Altered Carbon style)
Shift = jump in time on purpose; return with changes
Isekai = one-way trip to another world; you are still you
(not “start already as a new OC”)

────────────────────────────────
14. WERE / MER 🐺🌊
Optional transformation rules:
daylight, every night, anger, feeding, permanent effects.
You configure what is true in your story.

────────────────────────────────
15. SAFETY & QoL 🛡️
Information Firewall = characters don’t know meta / out-of-scene stuff
Standard Rules = baseline “write clean, keep pronouns straight…”
Your own extra rules: cards named Aetherheart Rules, Aetherheart Rules 2…
Silent Mode = system messages don’t spam the story
/save and /load = move your state between adventures (coded block)
/reset memory vs /reset all
Game Over screen if HP hits 0

────────────────────────────────
16. HOW TO TALK TO IT
You usually don’t need commands.
Talk like a person:
“check the job board”
“equip the leather vest”
“rest until morning”
“take the bounty on Vex”

Commands are there when you want a clean dump:
/status /inventory /time /help /keywords

────────────────────────────────
17. FREE / LOW CONTEXT
Scripts themselves don’t eat your context budget the same way
story text does.
Aetherheart tries to inject only what’s needed.
Turn OFF what you don’t use.
That’s the point.

────────────────────────────────
18. IF SOMETHING FEELS BROKEN
1. Is the system ON in Configure?
2. Did you wait one turn after changing a toggle?
3. /status and /help
4. /jailbreak for troubleshooting (suspends story pressure)
5. /reset memory if only memories are weird

────────────────────────────────
You didn’t need to memorize this.
Turn on one system.
Play.
Turn on the next when you want it.
